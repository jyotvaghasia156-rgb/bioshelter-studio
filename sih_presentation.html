Solution Highlights & Uniqueness
  • Parametric Bioclimatic Digital Twin: Real-time simulation of building orientation, WWR, roof pitch, wind towers, and diurnal solar radiation arcs.
  • Transient Energy Solver: Calculates heat flux ($Q = U \cdot A \cdot \Delta T$), decrement factor ($\mu = 0.22$), and phase delay ($\phi = 8.4\text{ hrs}$) for rammed earth/superadobe.
  • Interactive World Map → Studio Sync: 1-click climate ingestion from global weather stations directly into the 3D thermal model.
  • Multi-Auth Identity Gateway: Verified responder access via Phone SMS OTP, Google OAuth SSO, and instant Guest Explorer mode.
