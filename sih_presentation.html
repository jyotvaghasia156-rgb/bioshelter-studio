Kusuda Geothermal Subsoil Equation:
T(z, t) = T_mean - A_s • exp(-z √(π / α τ)) • cos(2π/τ (t - t_0 - z/2 √(τ / π α)))

Quantifies subsoil thermal damping and phase shift as a function of depth (z), soil thermal diffusivity (α), and annual period (τ = 365 days).

Fanger PMV Thermal Comfort Index:
PMV = [0.303 • exp(-0.036 M) + 0.028] • L_thermal

Iteratively solves mean radiant temperature ($T_{mrt}$), metabolic load ($M$), clothing insulation ($I_{cl}$), and boundary convective coefficients ($h_c$).
